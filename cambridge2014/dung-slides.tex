\begin{frame}{Against WUNG}

\textit{A fine tuning argument}

\bigskip Among all of the possible universes, the ``life supporting''
or ``biophilic'' universe make up an incredibly small subset.  If our
universe were only a little bit different than it is, then sentient
life would not have been possible.  In a universe that arose by
chance, most likely life would not have been possible.  But life is
possible in our universe.  Therefore our universe did not come about
by chance.

\[ p(N|\neg C)\approx 0,\qquad p(N)=1 \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad p(C)\approx 1 .\]

\begin{frame}{PART II: Against UNA}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Physical cosmology entails that the universe was not created by
  God.  (SUNA)
\item Physical cosmology provides evidence that the universe was not
  created by God.  (WUNA)
\end{enumerate}

\end{frame}


\begin{frame}{Against SUNA}

  \textit{A caricature of Quentin Smith's cosmological argument for
    atheism}


  \bigskip Premise 1: Belief that $P$ is justified only if $P$ figures
  in our best scientific explanations.

  \smallskip Premise 2: General relativity provides the best
  explanation of the origin and large-scale development of our
  universe.

  \smallskip Premise 3: General relativity doesn't say that God
  created the universe.

  \smallskip Conclusion: The claim that God created the universe is
  unjustified.



\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Against SUNA}

  Why might $P$ be absent from the best scientific explanation?

\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is false.
\item $P$ is true, but lacks a feature we prize in good scientific explanations.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $P$ is not fundamental.
\item $P$ is fundamental, but lawless. \end{enumerate} \end{enumerate}

\end{frame}


\begin{frame}{Against WUNA}

  \textit{A caricature of Sean Carroll's no-tuner argument}

  \bigskip If I forget that I know anything about the universe, and if
  I imagine that naturalism is true, then I would expect to see
  something very much like what we actually find.  But if I imagine
  that Theism is true, then I would expect to find a very different
  universe.  Therefore, the empirical evidence confirms naturalism.

\bigskip 
\[ 
  pr(E|\neg C) \: > \: pr(E|C) \]

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{PART III: Agnosticism}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Did \emph{not} say that the evidence on the two sides balances
  out.
\item Did not recommend that you alter your credence in $C$, unless
  your credence was tethered to the facts of physical cosmology.
\item Other, non-scientific considerations are more relevant for
  grappling with $C$.
\item A scientific naturalist ought to treat $C$ as meaningless, not
  as a failed attempt at a scientific explanation --- for Theism takes
  $C$ as a statement in metalanguage, not in the object language of
  natural science. \end{enumerate}


\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Conclusions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Part I: cosmology based natural theology fails
\item Part II: cosmology based natural atheology fails
\item Part III: agnosticism not mandated by Parts I and II
\item Recommendation for naturalists: remain silent about $C$
\item Recommendation for Theists: find the truth in NOMA
\end{itemize}
