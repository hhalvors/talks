---
title: Equivalence - State of Play
---

In this talk, I give an overview of definitions, results, and open
questions about theoretical equivalence. In particular, I consider a
spectrum of notions of equivalence from extremely liberal (coarse
grained) to extremely conservative (fine grained). I then go on to
discuss the metaphysical upshot of the "formal" theory of equivalence,
paying special attention to questions raised by Babic, Calosi, and
Teitel.
